Calc 4. Km per year and services

The counter-intuitive one

Very low mileage is not automatically good. A car that stood for long stretches or only did short cold trips can be in worse shape than a highway car: perished seals, tired battery, oil never reaching temperature, rusty brake discs. Under 8,000 km a year, ask why, and check services were done by time, not only km. Check the registration date on the papers.

Calc 5. The hill test (Nm per tonne)

Torque against weight predicts hill behaviour better than engine size. Both numbers are in the listing's spec tab (kerb weight in kg).
