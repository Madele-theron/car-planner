Calibrated against a real South African bank estimate at 13.40% over 72 months. Standard bank vehicle finance has no credit life (WesBank lists it as an optional extra), so the default here has none; the optional-credit-life route is modelled at R3 per R1,000 of outstanding balance. The initiation fee of R1,207.50 is added to the loan and the R69 monthly service fee is included in every instalment. Credit life is charged on the balance, so it falls as you pay down — which is why paying more than the minimum helps twice.
