Same engine as the Money Model: the R1,207.50 initiation fee is added to the loan, the R69 monthly service fee rides on every instalment, and credit life at R3 per R1,000 is charged on the balance still outstanding. Interest is charged on the balance too, so anything you pay above the instalment comes straight off the capital and stops being charged interest the very next month. There is no early-settlement penalty on a National Credit Act agreement, though you must give notice to settle in full — confirm the settlement quote with your bank before you pay it.
